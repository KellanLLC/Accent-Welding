## 2. Brand identity (already exists, do not reinvent)

A real logo system is already in use across the business card, the price list, and
both service flyers. The site should adopt it, not replace it.

**Wordmark** ACCENT WELDING, set in a heavy western/Tuscan slab display face with an
inline highlight and a hard drop shadow. Flared serifs, condensed caps.

**Mark** A longhorn steer skull, front-facing, with long sweeping horns. "20" and
"25" sit either side of it, flanked by tapered gold rules.

**Lockup** Skull + year over wordmark, with `★ STRENGTH IN STEEL ★` on a downward
arc beneath, three stars spaced along it.

**Two variants, both captured:**
- Light on dark: cream/white wordmark, tan skull, on near-black. (`fb-logo-banner.png`, 3434×1038 PNG)
- Bronze on white: for print on light stock. (`fb-01-pricelist-UPRIGHT.jpg`, and both IG flyers)

## Gaps I need you to fill

Short list. These are the things I genuinely cannot determine and that will change what
gets built.

**Blocking, needed before the site can ship**

1. **Which city?** The address is "719 East Center Street, 84655" with no city. ZIP 84655
   is shared by **Genola** and **Santaquin** (and a sliver of Payson). Both have an East
   Center Street. Local search ranking depends on getting this right and consistent.
2. **Which phone is primary?** `801-691-3685` is on the card and price list.
   `385-241-4679` is on both flyers. One should lead the site; the other can be secondary
   or dropped.
3. **Is the garden box price list current?** Published prices on a website are a
   commitment. Confirm, or I show "from $420" and route to a quote form.
4. **Licensed and insured?** Utah contractor licence number, general liability, workers'
   comp. This is the top trust signal for a business with zero reviews and it belongs in
   the footer.

**Important, shapes the content**

5. **Hours**, and whether you take calls after hours or on Saturdays.
6. **Service radius.** "Utah County and surrounding areas" is the stated line. How far
   will you actually travel, and is there a mileage charge?
7. **Photos of gates.** Gates are in the bio and on the card but there is not a single
   dedicated gate photo in the set. If gates are a real service they need a portfolio row.
8. **Lead time.** "Fast turnaround" is claimed but never quantified. A real number
   ("most railing jobs installed within 2 to 3 weeks") beats the adjective.
9. **Any testimonials.** Zero reviews on Facebook, none anywhere else. Even two or three
   named quotes from finished jobs would change the page. Also worth asking All Home
   Services for one.
10. **The full Facebook intro sentence**, which Facebook cuts off at "…stronger and m".

**Nice to have**

11. Higher-resolution originals of anything you shot on a phone. What I pulled is
    compressed to 1536px by Facebook; your camera roll will have the full files, and for
    a full-bleed hero the difference is visible.
12. The garden box carousel photos from the 15 May Instagram post, which are login-walled.
13. Whether you want the site to sell garden boxes directly, or just quote them.

**One flag, not a question:** [Edmonds Railing](https://www.edmondsrailings.com/) is in
Santaquin and has been fabricating custom railings since 1993. If you are in the same
town, they are the direct competitor and the site should be positioned deliberately
against 30 years of incumbency rather than pretending it is an empty market.
